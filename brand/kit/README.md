# Brio AI Brand Kit — "Ink & Paper"

The Sticker Asterisk: a playful multi-petal asterisk (yellow #F4C744, blue #3D52B4, pink #E56D93, navy #1E2438 center) on warm cream #FAF7F0.

## Contents

- `logo/svg/` — vector source: full color, single-color navy, single-color white
- `logo/png/` — raster exports at 256 / 512 / 768 px (1x / 2x / 3x)
- `favicon/` — favicon.ico (16+32+48), PNGs at 16/32/48, and 180 px apple-touch-icon
- `social/` — Open Graph image (1200×630), Instagram post (1080×1080) & story (1080×1920), X header (1500×500), LinkedIn banner (1584×396)
- `print/` — business card front/back, 3.5×2 in at 300 dpi (add 0.125 in bleed when sending to print)
- `tokens/` — design tokens as CSS custom properties, Tailwind config, and SCSS variables
- `cadence-brand-book.pdf` — the full brand guidelines book

## Quick rules

- Fraunces for display headings only; Inter for body/UI (min 16px web, 14px mobile).
- Clear space around the mark: one petal height. Minimum mark size: 24px.
- Cream dominates; navy is ink (text + CTAs); blue anchors; accents are sparing pops.
- Single-color mark for print, embossing, and watermarks.
